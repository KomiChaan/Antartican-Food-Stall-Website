Mango Lassi

Refreshing yogurt drink blended with ripe mangoes and a hint of cardamom. Sweet, cool, and oh-so-comforting.

How We Make It

  • Fresh, ripe mangoes are peeled and diced for maximum sweetness.
  • Blended with creamy yogurt, a touch of sugar, and a pinch of cardamom.
  • Served chilled, topped with a sprinkle of pistachios or saffron (optional).
  • Perfect for cooling off and refreshing your day!
