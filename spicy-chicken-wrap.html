Spicy Chicken Wrap

Juicy grilled chicken, fresh veggies, and our signature spicy sauce wrapped in a warm flatbread. A burst of flavor in every bite!

How We Make It

  • Fresh chicken breast is marinated overnight in a blend of yogurt, chili, garlic, and house spices.
  • Grilled to perfection for a smoky, juicy flavor.
  • Wrapped with crisp lettuce, tomatoes, onions, and a drizzle of our signature spicy sauce.
  • All rolled up in a warm, soft flatbread, made fresh daily.
  • Served hot and ready to enjoy!
