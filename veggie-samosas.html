Veggie Samosas

Crispy pastry filled with seasoned potatoes and peas, served with tangy chutney. Perfect for sharing and smiles!

How We Make It

  • Potatoes and peas are cooked with cumin, coriander, turmeric, and a hint of chili for a flavorful filling.
  • The mixture is wrapped in thin pastry sheets and folded into classic samosa triangles.
  • Each samosa is deep-fried until golden and crispy.
  • Served hot with our homemade tangy chutney.
