Sweet Churros

Golden-fried churros dusted with cinnamon sugar, served with chocolate dip. Happiness in every crunch!

How We Make It

  • Choux pastry dough is piped into hot oil and fried until golden and crisp.
  • Freshly fried churros are rolled in a blend of cinnamon and sugar.
  • Served with a rich, warm chocolate dipping sauce.
  • Perfect for a sweet treat any time of day!
